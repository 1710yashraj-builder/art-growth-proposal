# ART Mechatronics x Buildanta Studios — Digital Growth Proposal

## Motion Reel

`motion-reel.html` is a 15-second, 16:9 motion graphics reel. Every frame is drawn live on a 2D canvas, and the soundtrack is synthesized with the Web Audio API (no image, video or audio files). Open it in a browser and press play (sound on). `motion-reel.mp4` is the same reel exported to video (1920×1080, 60 fps).

## ART Launch Film

`art-launch-film.html` is a 30-second, 16:9 pitch film for the ART Mechatronics proposal, made to open the meeting with Mr. Anurag Tiwari. It covers the delivered work (₹7,75,000+ market value), the two paths, AI search and the one-week launch, in the proposal's navy-and-gold brand with its own machine photos. `art-launch-film.mp4` is the 1080p60 export with sound.
